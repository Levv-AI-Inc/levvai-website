# Control desk (/home)

Mode: Operate. Visitor: anyone who signs in, most often hiring managers and programme admins starting their day. Task: see what is waiting, ask Nova, or start a request. Constraints: only real data in the sentence and figures; motion stays at press and hover feedback; the Nova conversation view is unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The desk opens on the same record header as the admin pages, so the product has one way of stating what is true: the caller's queue and the policy state in one computed sentence, one tabular figure, and Nova's prompt directly under the header. It refuses the default of a grey title over white cards.

OWN-WORLD: The exposure record, as recorded in `.impeccable/surfaces/app-admin-layout-tsx.md` and DESIGN.md: paper ground, the zone ramp, Helvetica, tracked-capital legends, square corners, Levv Blue as the one accent; the Nova prompt, start links and Nova brief sit on the surface with 1px rules, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees at once whether anything of theirs is waiting and whether a policy governs Nova, then asks Nova or starts a request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The trail "Home" and the zone wedge; the title "Control desk" and the sentence beneath; the waiting figure; the Nova prompt full width under the header; the two start links side by side; the Nova brief under them.

FORM: Exposure record, shared with the admin settings surface (it replaced the earlier policy band); seed:01aac8661fa91b6b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
